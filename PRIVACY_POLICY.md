## Introduction

Home Clinic ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Home Clinic WhatsApp Booking Agent ("Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our booking platform via WhatsApp.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:
- **Personal Information:** Name, phone number (WhatsApp ID), address, pin code
- **Health Information:** Symptoms, medical conditions, and service preferences
- **Booking Details:** Date, time, preferred doctor/provider, service type
- **Communication Data:** Messages exchanged through WhatsApp for appointment booking

We also collect automatically:
- Message timestamps and metadata
- Device information
- IP addresses through our webhook logs

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:
- Create and manage your booking appointments
- Match you with qualified healthcare providers
- Send appointment confirmations and reminders
- Provide customer support and resolve issues
- Improve our service and user experience
- Comply with legal obligations
- Detect and prevent fraud or misuse

## 3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
- Database encryption at rest
- Secure API endpoints with signature verification
- Access controls and authentication (JWT tokens)
- Regular security monitoring

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your information for:
- **Active Bookings:** Duration of appointment plus 30 days
- **Completed Bookings:** Up to 2 years for medical records and audit
- **Messages:** Stored in our database for support and service improvement
- **Upon Request:** You can request deletion of your account and associated data

## 5. Data Sharing

We share your information only with:
- **Healthcare Providers:** Limited to appointment details (name, symptoms, time)
- **Service Partners:** Payment processors for booking transactions
- **Legal Authorities:** When required by law or regulation
- **Meta/WhatsApp:** Only message metadata (never sensitive health data)

We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties.

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your data (Right to be Forgotten)
- Opt-out of communications
- Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact: **privacy@homeclinic.local**

## 7. Cookies and Tracking

Our WhatsApp service does not use cookies. However, our website may use cookies for analytics and user experience improvement. You can control cookie settings in your browser.

## 8. Third-Party Links

Our service may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies independently.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If we discover such data, we will delete it immediately.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via WhatsApp or email. Continued use of our service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
